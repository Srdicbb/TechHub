@63f0b04059
`Parallel.For`, `Parallel.ForEachAsync` and PLINQ split a collection into parts and run them on several cores. How the work is split decides how long the cores sit idle.
@d53d2eb0e3

@3e8c6d1943

@49dbcd4cb0

@9c84509843

@1ed78cc134

@093415a079
