@f9f3d01be0

Kafka: partitions and offsets
@38bafc6471
Kafka keeps events in a **log split into partitions**. A message that has been read is not deleted: every **consumer group** remembers its own offset and reads at its own pace, so the same stream can be read again.
@d6e3797340
A partition is an append-only log. Ordering holds within a partition, not across the whole topic.
@b1663b2a06
The message key selects the partition (a hash of the key), so all events of one customer stay in order.
@56f1b95f8f
Within a group, a partition is read by at most one consumer: more consumers than partitions means some sit idle.
@bc4908490a
Different groups are independent: each has its own offset and its own lag.
@2b082e6a17
